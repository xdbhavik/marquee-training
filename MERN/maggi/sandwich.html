Cheese Veggie Sandwich

  1. Take two slices of fresh bread.
  2. Spread butter on one side of each slice.
  3. Spread generous green chutney on the buttered sides.
  4. Place a cheese slice (or grated cheese) on one slice.
  5. Add thinly sliced veggies - cucumber, onion, capsicum, and boiled potato if you like.
  6. Sprinkle little chaat masala or black salt (swad anusaar).
  7. Cover with the second slice, press gently.
  8. Optional: Toast or grill the sandwich till golden and cheese melts slightly.
  9. Cut into triangles or halves. Serve hot and enjoy!
