Poha Recipe

  1. Wash 1 cup poha (flattened rice) in water and drain. Let it sit for 5 mins.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chilli, and peanuts.
  3. Add chopped onion and sauté till soft.
  4. Add turmeric powder and salt (swad anusaar).
  5. Add the soaked poha and mix gently. Cook for 3-4 minutes.
  6. Squeeze half lemon and mix well.
  7. Turn off the heat, cover and let it rest for 2 mins.
  8. Serve hot, topped with sev and fresh pomegranate seeds. Enjoy!
