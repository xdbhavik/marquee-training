Maggi Recipe

  1. Bring water to a boil.
  2. Add maggi masala, and other spices (Swad anusaar)
  3. Add vegies if required.
  4. Add maggi.
  5. Let it cook until the maggi becomes soft enough.
  6. Cheese!
  7. Turn the heat off, cover the pot, and let the maggi cook for 5 mins.
  8. Top it with extra masala if you wish, Serve and Enjoy!
